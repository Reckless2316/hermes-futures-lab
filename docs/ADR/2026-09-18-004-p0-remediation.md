1. Candidate.1 and candidate.2 remain byte-identical. Candidate.3 supersedes
   candidate.2. All three retain their immutable pending metadata; P0 acceptance
   does not promote an evaluation manifest.
2. Encode drawdown basis as
   `max(initial_balance, highest_prior_session_closing_balance)`. With no prior
   closes, use initial balance. At session start only, the floor is
   `min(initial_balance, basis - drawdown_amount)`. A losing first close of
   49000 leaves the next floor at 48000, never 47000. This repairs manifest
   completeness and agrees with the existing ADR 002 formula.
3. Declare FTMO counting classes explicitly: standard 1.0, mini 1.0, micro 0.1.
   Sum absolute net holdings per contract times the class weight; no offset
   across contracts. Official examples distinguish 4 standard + 10 micro from
   2 mini + 30 micro. They do not verify how partial groups of ten micros count.
4. Fractional micro summation (one micro = 0.1, 51 micros = 5.1) is the current
   LAB CONVENTION, pending FTMO confirmation. No inferred rounding or grouping
   behavior is promoted to an official rule. Reports and synthetic vectors must
   retain unresolved official semantics.
5. Report both gross and net-of-fees consistency shares. The net basis remains
   the human-accepted ADR 003 lab convention pending official verification.
   Preserve independently computed numerators, denominators, shares and results;
   never silently substitute one for the other. Fees may change the best day or
   make just the net denominator nonpositive. DATA_CONTRACT specifies the fields.
6. Pin the validation interpreter, lock both test runners' dependencies, and
   exercise `uv sync --locked` in a fresh checkout/environment before reporting
   results. Tests validate P0 contracts; they are not a financial evaluator.
7. The repository is public. The human selected MIT on 2026-09-18. Historical
   publication approval documents a past action; it grants no permission for
   future unrelated actions. This request authorizes the PR #2 remediation,
   branch update and re-review handoff only.
